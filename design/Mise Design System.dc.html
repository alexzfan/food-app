Garlic Butter Noodles

15 MIN SERVES 2 EASY

Ingredients

2
200 gdried spaghetti
4 clovesgarlic, thinly sliced
3 tbspunsalted butter
½ tspchili flakes
30 gparmesan, grated
handfulparsley, chopped

Method

1

Bring a pot of well-salted water to the boil and cook the spaghetti until just shy of al dente. Reserve a mug of pasta water before draining.

2

Meanwhile, melt the butter over medium heat until it foams. Add the garlic and chili flakes and cook gently until fragrant and just golden — don't let the garlic brown.

3

Add the drained pasta to the pan with a splash of pasta water and the parmesan. Toss hard over the heat until glossy and emulsified, then fold through the parsley.

4

Taste, season with sea salt and black pepper, and serve immediately with an extra grating of parmesan.
